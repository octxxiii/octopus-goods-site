Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026 · Applies to Goods: Last, Goods: Routine, Goods: Maintain, Goods: Together, Goods: Purchase

Overview

The Goods apps are local-first iOS apps. Each app stores what you enter — items, completion history, notes, and photos — on your device. We do not run our own servers that collect or process your data, and we do not sell your data or show third-party ads.

Goods: Last

Stores your tracked items and completion timestamps on-device. You can export and import a JSON backup yourself; that file never leaves your device unless you share it. Home Screen widget data is shared with the app only through Apple's App Group storage on your device.

Goods: Routine

Stores your routines and run history on-device. Live Activity and Apple Watch features exchange the current routine state only between your own iPhone and your own paired devices, through Apple's system frameworks.

Goods: Maintain

Stores the items, parts, care history, and any photos you attach on-device. Nothing is uploaded to us.

Goods: Together

Shared chore lists and completion history are synced through your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, to the people you invite. This data is stored in your iCloud, governed by Apple's privacy policy, not on our servers.

Goods: Purchase

Receipt photos you capture or import are processed on-device with Apple's on-device text recognition (OCR) to read the amount, store, and date. Receipt images, purchase records, and generated claim PDFs stay on your device.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment details; Apple's privacy policy covers that transaction.

Diagnostics

The apps may log basic usage events locally (such as which screen was opened) to help us debug crashes you report to us directly. These logs stay on your device unless you choose to share them with us.

Your Choices

You can delete any app's data by deleting the app, or delete individual items from within the app. Together data shared through iCloud can be removed by leaving the shared space or deleting your iCloud data for that app.

Contact

For privacy requests, email octxxiiistudio@gmail.com or visit our support page.
