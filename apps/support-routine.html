Restoring a purchase

Open the app's settings screen and use “Restore Purchases.” Restoring requires signing in to the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Your data

Routine stores your routines and run history on your device. Live Activity and Apple Watch features exchange the current routine state only between your own iPhone and your own paired devices, through Apple's system frameworks.
