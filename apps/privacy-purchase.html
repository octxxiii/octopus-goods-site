Purchase Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Overview

Purchase is a local-first iOS app. Receipt photos you capture or import are processed on-device with Apple's on-device text recognition (OCR) to read the amount, store, and date. Receipt images, purchase records, and generated claim PDFs stay on your device. Purchase does not run its own servers, does not collect analytics beyond local device logs, and does not sell data or show third-party ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment details; Apple's privacy policy covers that transaction.

Your Choices

You can delete your data by deleting individual items from within the app, or by deleting the app itself.

Contact

For privacy requests, email octxxiiistudio@gmail.com or visit our support page.
