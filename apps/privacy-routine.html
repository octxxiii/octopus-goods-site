Routine Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Overview

Routine is a local-first iOS app. Routine stores your routines and run history on-device. Live Activity and Apple Watch features exchange the current routine state only between your own iPhone and your own paired devices, through Apple's system frameworks. Routine does not run its own servers, does not collect analytics beyond local device logs, and does not sell data or show third-party ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment details; Apple's privacy policy covers that transaction.

Your Choices

You can delete your data by deleting individual items from within the app, or by deleting the app itself.

Contact

For privacy requests, email octxxiiistudio@gmail.com or visit our support page.
