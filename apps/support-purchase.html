Restoring a purchase

Open the app's settings screen and use “Restore Purchases.” Restoring requires signing in to the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Your data

Purchase stores your receipt photos, purchase records, and generated claim PDFs on your device. Export and import a JSON backup from Settings before deleting or reinstalling.
