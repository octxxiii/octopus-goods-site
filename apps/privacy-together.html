Together Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Overview

Together is a local-first iOS app. Shared chore lists and completion history are synced through your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, to the people you invite. This data is stored in your iCloud, governed by Apple's privacy policy, not on our servers. Together does not collect analytics beyond local device logs, and does not sell data or show third-party ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment details; Apple's privacy policy covers that transaction.

Your Choices

You can delete your data by deleting individual items from within the app, or by deleting the app itself.

Contact

For privacy requests, email octxxiiistudio@gmail.com or visit our support page.
