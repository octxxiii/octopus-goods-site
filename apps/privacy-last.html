Last Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Overview

Last is a local-first iOS app. Last stores your tracked items and completion timestamps on-device. You can export and import a JSON backup yourself; that file never leaves your device unless you share it. Home Screen widget data is shared with the app only through Apple's App Group storage on your device. Last does not run its own servers, does not collect analytics beyond local device logs, and does not sell data or show third-party ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment details; Apple's privacy policy covers that transaction.

Your Choices

You can delete your data by deleting individual items from within the app, or by deleting the app itself.

Contact

For privacy requests, email octxxiiistudio@gmail.com or visit our support page.
