Restoring a purchase

Open the app's settings screen and use “Restore Purchases.” Restoring requires signing in to the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Your data

Together keeps your shared chore list and completion history in your own iCloud account, synced through Apple's CloudKit to the people you invite. Reinstalling the app keeps your shared space as long as you sign back in with the same Apple ID.

Bug reports and feature requests
