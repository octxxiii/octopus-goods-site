Restoring a purchase

Open the app's settings screen and use “Restore Purchases.” Restoring requires signing in to the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Your data

Last stores your tracked items and completion timestamps on your device. Export and import a JSON backup from Settings before deleting or reinstalling — that file never leaves your device unless you share it. Home Screen widget data is shared with the app only through Apple's App Group storage on your device.

Bug reports and feature requests
