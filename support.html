Support

Support for Goods: Last, Goods: Routine, Goods: Maintain, Goods: Together, Goods: Purchase

Contact

Email octxxiiistudio@gmail.com with the app name, your iOS version, and what happened. We reply from the same address.

Restoring a purchase

Open the app's settings screen and use “Restore Purchases.” Restoring requires signing in to the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Recovering your data

Goods: Last, Maintain, and Purchase support JSON backup and restore from within the app — export a backup before deleting or reinstalling the app. Goods: Together keeps shared data in iCloud, so reinstalling keeps your shared space as long as you sign back in with the same Apple ID.

Bug reports and feature requests

Send these to the same support email above. Screenshots or screen recordings help us reproduce issues faster.

Privacy

See the privacy policy for what each app stores and how it is used.
